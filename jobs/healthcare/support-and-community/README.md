# Support & Community templates for Healthcare

Triage tickets, answer customers and moderate communities. 12 Grok Bot templates, 11 of them filed in this folder; the others live under their main field and are linked from here.

| Template | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Adherence Gap Outreach](adherence-gap-outreach.md) | Finds lapsed refills, drafts reason-matched outreach, and tracks replies until the patient refills or opts out. |
| [Cohort Engagement Re-Engager](cohort-engagement-re-engager.md) | Finds quiet members in your group program and drafts personal re-engagement messages for your approval. |
| [Critical Incident Peer Support Follow-up](critical-incident-peer-support-follow-up.md) | Offers every crew member on a critical call peer support at set points, with a contact log only. |
| [Dry Eye Treatment Follow-Up](dry-eye-treatment-follow-up.md) | Keeps dry eye patients on treatment and flags when the plan needs changing. |
| [Healthcare Patient Support](../../customer-support/support-and-community/healthcare-patient-support.md) | Handles patient billing, insurance, appointment and complaint questions with empathy and clear escalation. |
| [New Client Intake and Eligibility Assistant](new-client-intake-and-eligibility-assistant.md) | Screens new midwifery inquiries, checks eligibility, gathers records, and books first visits. |
| [Patient Complaint Case Handler](patient-complaint-case-handler.md) | Logs, routes and drafts answers to patient complaints so every point is answered on time. |
| [Patient Inquiry Handling Assistant](patient-inquiry-handling-assistant.md) | Handles patient inquiries about appointments, billing, insurance, and records for medical billers. |
| [Patient Portal Message Triage](patient-portal-message-triage.md) | Routes every incoming patient portal message to the right queue and keeps it moving until answered. |
| [Post-Operative Check-In Assistant](post-operative-check-in-assistant.md) | Sends timed post-op check-ins, flags warning answers, and routes patients to staff for same-day calls. |
| [Postpartum Check-in Assistant](postpartum-check-in-assistant.md) | Sends scheduled postpartum check-ins, scores answers for warning signs, and alerts the midwife. |
| [Risk Flag Escalation Assistant](risk-flag-escalation-assistant.md) | Reads client check-ins for risk signals, drafts escalations, and tracks follow-up until the professional responds. |
