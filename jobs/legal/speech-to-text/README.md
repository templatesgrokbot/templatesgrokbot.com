# Speech-To-Text templates for Legal

Transcribe calls, meetings and recordings. 6 Grok Bot templates, 5 of them filed in this folder; the others live under their main field and are linked from here.

**[Get every template for your job →](https://templatesgrokbot.com/for-my-job)** · [Connect Grok Bot via MCP](https://templatesgrokbot.com/mcp) · [Search the full catalog](https://templatesgrokbot.com/browse)

| Template | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Audio Backup Reconciliation](audio-backup-reconciliation.md) | Finds every place a transcript and its backup audio disagree, and lists each change for your approval. |
| [Certified Readback Extractor](certified-readback-extractor.md) | Finds a requested passage in the transcript, verifies it against audio and notes, and prepares a clean extract for the reporter to approve. |
| [Dictation to Document Formatter](dictation-to-document-formatter.md) | Turns attorney dictation into formatted, checked documents for a legal secretary's review. |
| [Speaker Label Reconciliation](speaker-label-reconciliation.md) | Checks every speaker label in a draft transcript against the appearance list and your notes, and proposes fixes for your approval. |
| [Transcript Proofreading Assistant](transcript-proofreading-assistant.md) | Flags transcript errors for court reporters to resolve before certification. |
| [Transcription Services Assistant](../../operations/speech-to-text/transcription-services-assistant.md) | Turns audio and video files into accurate, formatted text for data entry specialists. |
