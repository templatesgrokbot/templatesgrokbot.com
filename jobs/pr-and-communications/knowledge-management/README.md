# Knowledge Management templates for PR and Communications

Notes, documents, PDFs and knowledge bases kept in order. 7 Grok Bot templates.

**[Get every template for your job →](https://templatesgrokbot.com/for-my-job)** · [Connect Grok Bot via MCP](https://templatesgrokbot.com/mcp) · [Search the full catalog](https://templatesgrokbot.com/browse)

| Template | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Announcement Consistency Checker](announcement-consistency-checker.md) | Keeps every version of an announcement saying the same thing after the master facts change. |
| [Crisis Holding Statement Readiness](crisis-holding-statement-readiness.md) | Keeps every crisis scenario's holding statement current, fact-checked and its approvers reachable. |
| [Intranet Failed Search Fixer](intranet-failed-search-fixer.md) | Finds the intranet searches that fail, drafts fixes, and reports whether search success improved. |
| [Intranet Freshness Steward](intranet-freshness-steward.md) | Keeps intranet pages current, owned, and free of broken links. |
| [Policy Change Rollout](policy-change-rollout.md) | Tracks down every old copy of a changed policy and confirms it is gone. |
| [Public Notice Version Sync](public-notice-version-sync.md) | Keeps every published format of a public notice showing the same current facts. |
| [Stakeholder Brief Update](stakeholder-brief-update.md) | Updates stakeholder briefs from documented interactions only, with dated revisions and flags for anything unverified. |
