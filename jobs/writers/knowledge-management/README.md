# Knowledge Management templates for Writers

Notes, documents, PDFs and knowledge bases kept in order. 11 Grok Bot templates, 7 of them filed in this folder; the others live under their main field and are linked from here.

**[Get every template for your job →](https://templatesgrokbot.com/for-my-job)** · [Connect Grok Bot via MCP](https://templatesgrokbot.com/mcp) · [Search the full catalog](https://templatesgrokbot.com/browse)

| Template | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Content Curator](../../operations/knowledge-management/content-curator.md) | Curates Obsidian vault content by detecting duplicates, stubs, and outdated notes. |
| [FAQ Gap Reviewer](faq-gap-reviewer.md) | Turns real recurring questions into source-linked FAQ updates and records the gaps. |
| [Help Center Freshness Audit](help-center-freshness-audit.md) | Monthly audit of help articles to produce a ranked fix list that keeps the help center accurate. |
| [Image Rights Register](image-rights-register.md) | Keeps a documented, licensed image register for your book so layout never stalls on rights. |
| [Ingest Youtube](../../operations/knowledge-management/ingest-youtube.md) | Pull a YouTube transcript into a markdown vault as a queryable note. |
| [Moc Agent](../../operations/knowledge-management/moc-agent.md) | Creates and maintains Obsidian Maps of Content to keep your vault navigable. |
| [Obsidian Markdown](../../education/knowledge-management/obsidian-markdown.md) | Create and edit Obsidian Flavored Markdown with wikilinks, callouts, and properties |
| [Obsidian Vault Assistant](obsidian-vault-assistant.md) | Builds and maintains your Obsidian vault: daily notes, meeting notes, links, and vault health checks. |
| [Script Notes Consolidation](script-notes-consolidation.md) | Gathers every script note, maps it to scenes, and tracks each one to resolution in the next draft. |
| [Story Archive Linking Assistant](story-archive-linking-assistant.md) | Finds relevant past coverage for new drafts and proposes links with background notes for editor approval. |
| [X Post to Markdown](x-post-to-markdown.md) | Converts X posts, threads and articles into clean markdown files with front matter. |
