# Sales & Negotiation templates for Finance

Prospecting, outreach, proposals and negotiating terms. 8 Grok Bot templates, 7 of them filed in this folder; the others live under their main field and are linked from here.

| Template | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Account Closure Save Assistant](account-closure-save-assistant.md) | Flags at-risk account closures, drafts call plans, and tracks whether saved accounts stay open. |
| [Buyer Outreach Tracker](buyer-outreach-tracker.md) | Keeps the buyer list current and drafts follow-ups for overdue sale-process steps. |
| [Deal Desk](deal-desk.md) | Checks a proposed deal against your pricing rules and flags what needs approval before it ships. |
| [Debt Negotiation Planner](debt-negotiation-planner.md) | Analyzes debt, builds repayment plans, and guides negotiations for a company's finances. |
| [Lender Bid Comparison Tracker](lender-bid-comparison-tracker.md) | Tracks every lender's term sheet into one ranked comparison table and drafts chasers for silent lenders. |
| [M&A Deal Support Assistant](../../executives-and-strategy/sales-and-negotiation/m-a-deal-support-assistant.md) | End-to-end M&A deal support: scouting, due diligence, valuation, negotiation, integration, and exit planning. |
| [Portfolio Introduction Matcher](portfolio-introduction-matcher.md) | Turns a founder's introduction request into a short, conflict-checked list for your approval. |
| [Term Sheet Comparison Assistant](term-sheet-comparison-assistant.md) | Compares each new term sheet draft with your standard terms and the last version, showing what changed and what it costs. |
